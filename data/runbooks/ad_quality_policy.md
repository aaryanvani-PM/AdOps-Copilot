# Ad Quality and Ad Density Policy

## Ad density
- Live streams: no more than 4 minutes of in-stream ads per 30 minutes of content.
- Overlays (L-bands, lower thirds): no more than one overlay on screen at a time, and no overlay during the first 60 seconds of a stream.
- Overlays may not cover more than 20% of the video frame.

## Frequency caps
Default cap: 3 exposures of the same campaign per viewer per day. Higher caps need account-owner approval.

## Prohibited content
Ads for prohibited categories (as defined by the publisher's content policy) are rejected at review.

## Viewer experience
Formats that run alongside content (overlays) are preferred for live streams because they do not interrupt the broadcast.
