# Pacing Troubleshooting

## Definitions
Pacing compares delivered impressions with the impressions a campaign should have delivered by today. Expected-to-date = goal impressions x (days elapsed / flight days). Pacing % = delivered / expected-to-date x 100.

## Thresholds
- On track: pacing between 90% and 110%.
- Underpacing: pacing below 90%. Investigate within one business day.
- Severely underpacing: pacing below 70%. Escalate to the account owner the same day.
- Overpacing: pacing above 110%. Check frequency caps and daily budget caps before the campaign exhausts its goal early.

## Common causes of underpacing
1. Narrow targeting (placement, geography, or audience) leaves too little eligible inventory.
2. Fill rate dropped on the placement, so fewer ad requests return an ad.
3. Creative was rejected or failed a format check, so the line item stopped serving.
4. Competing higher-priority line items are taking the same inventory.

## What to check, in order
1. Daily delivery trend for the last 7 days: is the shortfall steady or did it start on a specific date?
2. Fill rate on the campaign's placement for the same period.
3. Creative approval status.
4. Inventory forecast for the remaining flight.

## Remedies
Broaden targeting, add placements, or request a make-good (see the make-goods runbook). Never change a live campaign's goal without account-owner approval.
