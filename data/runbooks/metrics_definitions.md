# Metrics Definitions

- Impressions: number of times an ad was served and started.
- Viewers: unique viewers of the content during the campaign window.
- Ad viewers: viewers who saw at least one ad from the campaign.
- Ad reach rate: ad viewers / viewers. With SSAI, ads are stitched into the stream, so this is typically above 90%.
- Completion rate: completed ad views / impressions.
- Fill rate: ad requests that returned an ad / total ad requests.
- CPM: price per 1,000 impressions. Revenue = impressions / 1000 x CPM.
- eCPM: effective revenue per 1,000 impressions across all demand sources.
- Pacing: delivered impressions / expected impressions to date (see the pacing runbook).
