# Creative Rejections and Zero Delivery

## Symptom
A campaign that was delivering drops to zero impressions from a specific date.

## Most common cause
The creative was rejected or expired. Overlay formats (L-bands, lower thirds) must match the placement's safe-area and duration specs; a mismatch blocks serving.

## Checks
1. Confirm delivery dropped to exactly zero, not just low.
2. Check the creative approval status and expiry date.
3. Check whether the line item was paused.

## Fix
Ask the advertiser for a corrected creative. Once approved, delivery resumes within one hour. Log lost delivery days so a make-good can be calculated.

## Escalation
Zero delivery on an active campaign is always escalated to the account owner the same day.
