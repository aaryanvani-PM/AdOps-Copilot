# Escalation Policy

## Who handles what
- Ad-ops on-call: fill drops, SSAI or serving incidents, zero delivery.
- Account owner: pacing risks, make-goods, anything involving the advertiser.
- Finance: billing, invoices, and pricing questions.

## Response SLAs
- Live-stream serving incident: acknowledge within 30 minutes, resolve or update within 2 hours.
- Zero delivery on an active campaign: same business day.
- Underpacing below 70%: same business day.
- Underpacing between 70% and 90%: next business day.

## What an escalation must include
Campaign ID, advertiser, the metric that triggered it, when it started, what was already checked, and the requested action.

## Copilot rule
The copilot never takes actions. When a request needs an action or a decision, it escalates with the details above.
