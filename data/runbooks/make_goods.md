# Make-Goods

## When a make-good applies
A make-good is free delivery given to an advertiser when a campaign under-delivers against its goal because of a publisher-side issue (fill drop, serving incident, wrong creative setup on our side).

## When it does not apply
If under-delivery was caused by the advertiser (late or rejected creative, narrow targeting they requested), no make-good is owed.

## How it is calculated
Make-good impressions = goal impressions - delivered impressions, limited to the days affected by the publisher-side issue.

## Approval
Make-goods are approved by the account owner. The copilot can estimate the shortfall but cannot approve or promise a make-good.
